18 Principles of Manhood for Young Males

By John F. Bolden

Buy the book

The journey to manhood, at times, consists of more questions than answers. It can be frustrating and frightening without clear, direct, and honest conversations along the way. And without guidance, too many of our young males are slipping through the cracks.

The 18 Principles of Manhood for Young Males is more than just a book. It is a practical resource for men of all ages to use on their journey. We, as men, need more transparency about the transition from boyhood to manhood. Breaking the cultural norms will require courage, discipline, and focus.

“I pray that you are encouraged and pushed to grow and become more than what you have seen in society.”John F. Bolden, author

Books · Health & wellness

Black Health: Considered from a Holistic Perspective

By D'Andrea Monet Bolden, MA

Buy the book

Black Health was written for everyday people. It simplifies important research and health-related topics so they can be easily read by people without advanced degrees or a background in science, medicine, or mental health care.

In writing this book, D'Andrea Bolden set out to tackle the topic of health through the lens of Blackness. At the same time, she examines health from a multi-dimensional perspective, recognizing that many factors shape the state of a person's total health.

The conversation surrounding Black health can be messy, uncomfortable, and full of challenges. D'Andrea believes we can all learn and grow so that we can collectively see an improvement in the overall health of Black people. Throughout the book she shares scientific facts, personal stories, and other relevant information.

“Our health as black people is of great concern and many of us were not taught that black health is black wealth.”D'Andrea Bolden, author

Inside the book

  • Leading health conditions that are prevalent among the Black population
  • The negative effects poverty can have on health
  • How racism and stress can harm the health of Black people
  • Black maternal health issues
  • Black children and mental health
  • Health concerns for Black men
  • Concerns surrounding lead exposure and Black children
  • Holistic health

Kalamazoo 5th Grade Student Writes STEM-Focused Children's Chapter Book

Kalamazoo, MI — We are thrilled to announce the much-anticipated release of John B. and the Wonderful World of Coding, the debut book by first-time 11-year-old author and Kalamazoo native, John I. Bolden II. This captivating tale takes readers on an unforgettable journey of imagination, determination, and self-discovery.

In the book, readers are introduced to the main character, John B., a young boy full of creativity and big ideas. One day, John B. comes up with his biggest idea yet, but he is unsure how to make his idea a reality. With the support and guidance of his parents, John embarks on a life-changing adventure that will forever alter the course of his existence.

This heartwarming story serves as a reminder to keep pursuing your dreams and never give up. Through its pages, readers of all ages will be inspired to embrace their passions, overcome obstacles, and embark on their transformative journeys. "I want to inspire more kids to learn about coding," says John Bolden II, author and emerging entrepreneur.

John I. Bolden II wrote this book with contributions and help from his father John F. Bolden. "When John started his book I knew that my role was to be supportive and encourage him along the way," says John F. Bolden.

With an official release date of Tuesday, July 11th, John will host a virtual book launch on July 11th and an in-person book signing on July 15th, 1 pm – 3 pm in Kalamazoo, MI.

To learn more about John I. Bolden II, his upcoming appearances and his future literary endeavors, visit www.johnbcodes.com.

For media inquiries, please contact Izzy at writtenwisdombooks@gmail.com

About

Publishing the authors others overlook

At Written Wisdom Press we endeavor to publish cutting-edge books written by diverse authors all around the world. Although there is already a vast selection of books on the market, there is still a lack of diversity in the world of book publishing, and we endeavor to be a solution to this problem.

We are intentional in our efforts to highlight authors that would typically be overlooked by other publishing companies. For us, this is a bonus because it also allows us to provide our readers with a fresh and unique offering of books.

Although we are in the business of publishing books at Written Wisdom Press, we place a strong emphasis on the necessity of our manuscripts to be well suited for the targeted audience.

How we work

About independent publishing

$0Cost to authors. We are not a vanity publisher.
18–24 moTypical time from submission to release
RoyaltiesPaid to authors on every book sold

As a traditional independent publisher, we carefully curate our selection of manuscripts and offer authors a contract, after which we handle the printing, publishing, and distribution of their books through booksellers and other retailers. We hold the exclusive rights to publish the author's work and pay them royalties based on the book's sales.

We maintain a selective approach and only publish a limited number of books each year. Please note that we are not a vanity publisher and do not offer publication in exchange for payment. The traditional publishing process typically takes 18–24 months from the time of manuscript submission to book release. This timeline encompasses several stages, such as manuscript evaluation, editing, cover design, typesetting, printing, and distribution, among others. However, the exact duration may vary depending on the nature of the manuscript, current market trends, and workload of our publishing house.

We publish a limited number of books each year and never charge authors to publish.

Because we review every submission carefully, please allow time for a response. The full process from submission to release typically takes 18–24 months.
